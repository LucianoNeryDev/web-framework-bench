About

I build web software and the infrastructure it runs on. This site collects what I learn along the way, from a one-line shell trick to a month-long migration.

Every post is written to be useful a year from now: what I tried, what broke, what the numbers said and what I would do differently.

If something here saved you an afternoon, or you think I got it wrong, write to ada@bench.example.
